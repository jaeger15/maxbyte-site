Who We Are

A Smarter Space for Every Creative Mind.

Founded in 2026 by five Informatics students at Bunda Mulia University, Maxbyte was created to redefine how people store and manage their digital assets.

Built for designers, freelancers, and students, we believe cloud storage should be practical, flexible, and affordable for creative workers. More than just a place to keep files, Maxbyte is designed to become part of your everyday workflow.
